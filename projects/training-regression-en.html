DM05 post-training regression and coverage audits

I run checkpoint evaluations with strict coverage checks, keeping policy outcomes, system errors, and the limits of historical comparisons distinct.

DM05 · LoRA · LIBERO · Regression

The question

Has a trained checkpoint been evaluated completely, and what conclusions does the result support?

My contribution

  • Contribute to post-training inference checks, four-suite LIBERO evaluation, and investigation of run-time issues.
  • Compare planned samples with observed unique episodes, checking duplicates, omissions, out-of-range entries, and system errors.
  • Keep checkpoint identity, protocol, and scores connected, avoiding improvement claims from uncontrolled historical comparisons.

Conditions and scope

DM05 LoRA checkpoint 30k; LIBERO Spatial, Object, Goal, and LIBERO-10 with 500 episodes each. The final strict audit covers 2,000 unique episodes.

How to interpret the result

The result belongs to this checkpoint and protocol. The historical base-model result is not a matched, controlled A/B comparison, so it does not establish a gain from LoRA training or a personal algorithmic contribution.

What I learned

Training, inference, and evaluation need a continuous evidence trail. Full coverage first answers whether the evaluation is complete; model-quality conclusions require an additional controlled comparison.
