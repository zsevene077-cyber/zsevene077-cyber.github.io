Multimodal evaluation and protocol diagnostics

I work on multimodal evaluation entry points, task configuration, and result checks, distinguishing functional scoring from strict, anomaly-free validation.

lmms-eval · VLM · Scoring · Audit

The question

Does a score from an evaluation script automatically mean the result is comparable?

My contribution

  • Help connect task adapters, dataset fields, inference calls, and scoring procedures.
  • Record protocol differences, anomalous samples, and coverage, separating functional completion from full coverage and strict acceptance.
  • Keep reusable task configuration, scoring logic, and audit notes separate from large prediction artifacts.

Conditions and scope

A functional validation across 32 evaluation entries, with different tested scopes per benchmark. Anomalous entries remain visible rather than being counted as strict passes merely because a score was produced.

How to interpret the result

Four of the 31 functionally completed entries retain anomalies; one additional entry remains pending. Coverage differs by task, so these are not 31 complete, formal, directly comparable benchmark results.

What I learned

Before interpreting a score, I want to know the model identity, sample scope, scoring protocol, and treatment of errors.
